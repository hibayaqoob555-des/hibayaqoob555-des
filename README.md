# Hi there! 👋 

I'm Hiba, a creative based in Pakistan, but my clock runs on Eastern Daylight Time (EDT). Currently working as a graphic designer for a US-based marketing agency.

I believe every design should tell a story. That's how you build brand advocates, not just one time customers. In a world where people tune out generic push marketing, they connect with brands that feel emotionally close to them. And once that connection is made, customers become advocates: they recommend the brand, go out of their way to buy from it, and are always first in line for the next launch.

I've worked on 100+ brands, but my favorite remains **[Cake Piece](https://hibayaqoob.framer.website/work-examples-gallery/cake-piece-brand-identity)**, a project that connected the brand owner directly with consumers by telling the right visual story to the right audience.
